How I got here.

Background

I came to software from the trading side. Running a prop firm meant living inside tools that were slow, wrong, or priced like enterprise contracts — so I started replacing them.

That means I write code with the domain already loaded: I know what a breach rule needs to catch, why a fill timestamp matters, and what a trader actually looks at when they open a dashboard at 3am.

Still early in the engineering craft and honest about it. Learning fast, shipping to production, and reading a lot of other people's code.
